# 12U Softball Practice Plan - 90 minutes

**Document Type:** Practice Plan
**Age Group:** 12U
**Skill Level:** Beginner to Intermediate
**Main Focus:** Fundamentals, Throwing, Fielding, Hitting, Base Running
**Last Updated:** May 2026

## Overview
- **Goal:** Build core softball fundamentals while keeping practice fun, active, and positive.
- **Equipment Needed:** Softballs (12-inch), bats, tees, gloves, bases/cones, batting helmets, fielding mask.
- **Coaching Emphasis:** High activity, short lines, positive reinforcement, safety.

## Detailed Practice Schedule

### 1. Arrival & Dynamic Warm-Up (0-15 min)
**Duration:** 15 minutes
**Activities:**
- Light jogging (2 laps around the field)
- Dynamic stretching: arm circles, leg swings, high knees, butt kicks, torso twists, wrist/shoulder rolls
- Recap last game(s) if necessary

**Coaching Notes:** Greet players, check energy and attendance.

### 2. Throwing & Catching (15-30 min)
**Duration:** 15 minutes
**Activities:**
- Partner throwing (progress from 15-20 feet to longer distances)
- Focus points: 4-seam grip, proper footwork, wrist snap, follow-through, accurate chest-high throws
- Crow hop throws and knee throws for mechanics
- Short-hop / glove flip drill

**Coaching Notes:** Emphasize mechanics and accuracy

### 3. Fielding Stations (30-50 min)
**Duration:** 20 minutes
**Format:** Rotate through 3 stations (4-5 minutes each)
**Stations:**
- Station 1: Ground Balls (alligator hands, field out front, stay low)
- Station 2: Fly Balls / Pop Fly (calling for ball, proper footwork)
- Station 3: Relay / Cutoffs (basic introduction)

**Coaching Notes:** Make it competitive with scoring or challenges.

#### 4. Hitting Stations (50-75 min)
**Duration:** 25 minutes
**Format:** Rotate through stations (keep the lines short)
**Stations:**
- Tee Work (8 min) - level swing, contact point, finish high
- Soft Toss (8 min) - timing and head still
- Front Toss of Live Batting (9 min)
- Bunting Practice (2-3 reps per player)

**Coaching Notes:** Use positive cues: "See the ball, hit the ball", "Hands inside the ball"

### 5. Base Running & Situations (75-85 min)
**Duration:** 10 minutes
**Activities:**
- Base running fundamentals: leads, rounding first, secondary leads
- Home-to-first springs
- Simple situational drills (runner on 1str, ground ball; fly ball tag-up)
- Base running relays

### 6. Team Huddle & Cool-Down (85-90 min)
**Duration:** 5 minutes
**Activities:**
- Light stretching
- Team discussion: What went well? One thing to improve?
- Positive shout-outs
- Team cheer

## General Coaching Guidelines
- Keep all lines moving and groups small
- Prioritize safety (helmets on for hitting, proper spacing)
- Use lot of praise and high-fives
- Adjust difficulty based on team skill level and experience
- Include water breaks as needed
- Always expect the unexpected
