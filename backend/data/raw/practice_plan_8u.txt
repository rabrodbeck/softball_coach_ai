# 8U Softball Practice Plan - 90 Minutes

**Document Type:** Practice Plan  
**Age Group:** 8U  
**Duration:** 90 minutes  
**Skill Level:** Beginner (mostly first-time players)  
**Main Focus:** Maximum Fun, Basic Fundamentals, Confidence Building, Team Enjoyment  
**Last Updated:** May 2026

## Overview
- **Goal:** Introduce softball in a fun, positive way. Teach very basic skills while prioritizing enjoyment, movement, and confidence. Keep everything simple and energetic.
- **Equipment Needed:** Softballs (11-inch or softer training balls), bats, tees (multiple), gloves, bases/cones, batting helmets, tennis balls (recommended for beginners).
- **Coaching Emphasis:** Very short explanations, frequent demonstrations, constant movement, tons of praise, short attention spans. Make every activity feel like a game.

## Detailed Practice Schedule

### 1. Arrival & Dynamic Warm-Up (0-15 min)
**Duration:** 15 minutes  
**Activities:**
- Fun jogging or animal walks around the field (1-2 laps)
- Dynamic stretches with games: arm circles, high knees, butt kicks, jumping jacks, “butterfly” stretches
- Silly team cheer or group chant

**Coaching Notes:** Greet every child warmly by name. Make them feel excited to be there.

### 2. Throwing & Catching Basics (15-30 min)
**Duration:** 15 minutes  
**Activities:**
- Partner throwing at very short distances (10-20 feet)
- Focus points: Basic grip, step toward target, gentle underhand or easy overhand throws
- Knee or seated throws for mechanics
- Partner soft toss and catch (use softer balls if needed)

**Coaching Notes:** Celebrate every successful catch. Keep it light — no pressure on perfect form.

### 3. Fielding Stations (30-50 min)
**Duration:** 20 minutes  
**Format:** Rotate through 3 fun stations (5-6 minutes each)  
**Stations:**
- Station 1: Ground Ball “Alligator Hands” (coach rolls slow balls, focus on getting low and squeezing)
- Station 2: Pop Fly Fun (use tennis balls or underhand lobs — practice calling “Mine!” or “I got it!”)
- Station 3: Rolling Relay or “Clean It Up” game (pick up balls quickly as a team)

**Coaching Notes:** Turn everything into a game. Use cheers and high-fives for every good effort.

### 4. Hitting Stations (50-75 min)
**Duration:** 25 minutes  
**Format:** Rotate through stations with very short lines  
**Stations:**
- Tee Work (12-15 min) – primary focus: basic stance, watching the ball, level swing, hit off the tee
- Soft Toss (5-8 min) – coach or parent feeds very slowly from the side
- Bunting or “Just Touch It” introduction (optional, 2-3 reps)

**Coaching Notes:** Use very simple cues: “Watch the ball”, “Swing smooth”, “Hit it hard!”. Celebrate every contact loudly.

### 5. Base Running & Fun Games (75-85 min)
**Duration:** 10 minutes  
**Activities:**
- Basic base running: running through first base, learning the bases in order
- Fun home-to-first sprints or relay races
- Simple “Run the Bases” game or tag games on the bases

**Coaching Notes:** Emphasize hustle and fun. Teach “run hard” and “high-five the coach at first base.”

### 6. Team Huddle & Cool-Down (85-90 min)
**Duration:** 5 minutes  
**Activities:**
- Quick group stretch or favorite silly stretch
- Team talk: “What was your favorite part today?”
- Positive shout-outs and smiles
- Big team cheer or fun ending chant

## General Coaching Guidelines
- Keep all activities short, fast-paced, and moving — minimize standing in lines
- Prioritize fun and safety above skill development
- Use softer balls and multiple tees to maximize reps and minimize waiting
- Give constant positive feedback and high-fives
- Adjust on the fly based on energy and attention levels
- Include frequent quick water breaks
- Focus on building love for the game rather than winning or perfect technique
