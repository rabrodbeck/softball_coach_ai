# 10U Softball Practice Plan - 90 Minutes

**Document Type:** Practice Plan
**Age Group:** 10U
**Duration:** 90 minutes
**Skill Level:** Beginner
**Main Focus:** Fun, Fundamentals, Basic Throwing, Fielding, Hitting, Base Running
**Last Updated:** May 2026

## Overview
- **Goal:** Teach core softball fundamentals in a fun, high-energy environment while building confidence and team spirit.
- **Equipment Needed:** Softballs (11-inch), bats, tees, gloves, bases/cones, batting helmets.
- **Coaching Emphasis:** Maximum fun, very short lines, constant movement, lots of praise, safety first. Keep explanations short and demonstrations clear.

## Detailed Practice Schedule

### 1. Arrival & Dynamic Warm-Up (0-15 min)
**Duration:** 15 minutes
**Activities:**
- Light jogging or fun run around the field (1-2 laps)
- Dynamic stretching: arm circles, leg swings, knee highs, butt kicks, jumping jacks, torso twists
- Team cheer or silly chant

**Coaching Notes:** Greet every player by name, make it welcoming and energetic.

### 2. Throwing & Catching (15-30 min)
**Duration:** 15 minutes
**Activities:**
- Partner throwing at shorter distnaces (15-20 feet)
- Focus points: Proper grip, step toward target, throwing elbow high, accurate throws to chest
- Knee throws for mechanics
- Simple partner ground ball rolls and fielding

**Coaching Notes:** Emphasize fun and good effor over perfection.  Praise loudly for correct form.

### 3. Fielding Stations (30-50 min)
**Duration:** 20 minutes
**Format:** Rotate through 3 stations (4-6 minutes each)
**Stations:*
- Station 1: Ground Balls (alligator hands, stay low, field in front)
- Station 2: Fly Balls / Pop Fly (use tennis balls if needed, focus on calling "Mine!")
- Station 3: Basic Infield Fun (rollers, quick throws to first base)

**Coaching Notes:** Turn stations into mini-games wtih points or cheers for clean fielding.

### 4. Hitting Stations (50-75 min)
**Duration:** 25 minutes
**Format:** Rotate through stations (keep lines short)
**Stations:**
- Tee Work (10 min) - focus on level swing, watching the ball, balanced stance
- Soft Toss (8 min) - coach/parent feeds from side; emphasize timing
- Front Toss or Machine Work (7 min)
- Bunting instruction (2-3 reps per player)

**Coaching Notes:** Use simple cues: "See the ball, hit the ball", "Swing nice and easy", "Finish your swing".  Celebrate every solid contact.

### 5. Base Running & Fun Situations (75-85 min)
**Duration:** 10 minuites
**Activities:**
- Base running basics: running through first base, rounding bases 
- Home-to-first sprints (make it a fun race)
- Simple situational drills: "Run or hit", "Stop at first"
- Base running relays or tag games

**Coaching Notes:** Keep it light and energetic.  Focus on hustle and fun.

### 6. Team Huddle & Cool-Down (85-90 min)
**Duration:** 5 minutes
**Activities:**
- Light stretching or team favorite stretching
- Team talk: What was fun today?  One thing we learned?
- End with big team dcheer or fun break call

## General Coaching Guidelines
- Keep groups small and everyone moving at all times
- Prioritize safety: helmets on for hitting, watch arm fatiguq, proper spacing
- Use tons of positive reinforcement and high-fives
- Adjust based on attention and energy levels
- Incorporate games and competitions whenever possible
- Schedule frequent quick water breaks
