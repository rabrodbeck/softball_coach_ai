# 14U Softball Practice Plan - 90 Minutes

**Document Type:** Practice Plan  
**Age Group:** 14U  
**Duration:** 90 minutes  
**Skill Level:** Intermediate to Advanced (2+ years experience)  
**Main Focus:** Skill refinement, Game Situations, Speed & Agility, Team Defense, Competitive Intensity  
**Last Updated:** May 2026

## Overview
- **Goal:** Refine fundamentals, develop advanced softball IQ, increase game-like intensity, and build mental toughness and team chemistry.
- **Equipment Needed:** Softballs (12-inch), bats, tees, pitching machine (optional), gloves, bases/cones, batting helmets, radar gun (optional).
- **Coaching Emphasis:** Higher intensity, competitive drills, detailed feedback, strategic thinking, efficiency. Hold players accountable while keeping it engaging.

## Detailed Practice Schedule

### 1. Arrival & Dynamic Warm-Up (0-15 min)
**Duration:** 15 minutes  
**Activities:**
- Jogging + dynamic warm-up (2-3 laps)
- Arm care routine: band work or wrist flips, shoulder stretches, arm circles
- Agility ladder or quick footwork drills (high knees, butt kicks, lateral shuffles)
- Team cheer / motivational chant

**Coaching Notes:** Check in with players on energy, soreness, and mindset. Emphasize proper arm care.

### 2. Throwing & Receiving (15-30 min)
**Duration:** 15 minutes  
**Activities:**
- Long toss progression (start at 30-40 feet, build distance)
- Focus points: 4-seam grip, crow hop mechanics, strong wrist snap, accurate throws on the move
- Quick glove transfers and short-hop drills
- Partner relay throws with cutoffs

**Coaching Notes:** Demand crisp footwork and strong, accurate throws. Correct mechanics immediately.

### 3. Fielding Stations (30-50 min)
**Duration:** 20 minutes  
**Format:** Rotate through 3 advanced stations (5-6 minutes each)  
**Stations:**
- Station 1: Ground Balls (forehand/backhand, charging slow rollers, double plays)
- Station 2: Fly Balls & Communication (gap coverage, calling priorities, diving emphasis)
- Station 3: Cutoffs & Relays (perfect throws to bases, backup positioning)

**Coaching Notes:** Add competition between groups. Focus on game-speed execution and decision making.

### 4. Hitting Stations (50-75 min)
**Duration:** 25 minutes  
**Format:** Rotate through stations with purposeful reps  
**Stations:**
- Tee Work / Dry Swings (6 min) – power mechanics, gap hitting, opposite field
- Soft Toss or Front Toss (7 min) – timing, adjust to pitch location
- Live Pitching or Machine Work (10 min) – situational hitting (hit & run, sacrifice, gap)
- Bunting & slap hitting (2-3 reps per player)

**Coaching Notes:** Use advanced cues: “Load and explode”, “Stay through the ball”, “Drive the ball”. Track quality at-bats.

### 5. Base Running & Game Situations (75-85 min)
**Duration:** 10 minutes  
**Activities:**
- Advanced leads, secondary leads, and steals
- Situational base running: reading contact, tagging up, aggressive rounding
- Live scenarios: “Runner on 2nd, 0 outs”, squeeze plays, first-and-third situations
- Base running sprints or competitive relays

**Coaching Notes:** Emphasize smart, aggressive base running and quick decision-making under pressure.

### 6. Team Huddle & Cool-Down (85-90 min)
**Duration:** 5 minutes  
**Activities:**
- Light stretching and arm recovery
- Team discussion: What worked well? One area to improve?
- Positive shout-outs and leadership highlights
- Team cheer / break

## General Coaching Guidelines
- Maintain high energy and competitive focus throughout
- Keep lines moving but allow for deeper instruction where needed
- Prioritize safety with proper warm-up and arm care
- Incorporate leadership opportunities for older players
- Use video review or quick feedback sessions when possible
- Adjust intensity based on upcoming games or fatigue levels
- Balance skill work with mental game and team bonding
