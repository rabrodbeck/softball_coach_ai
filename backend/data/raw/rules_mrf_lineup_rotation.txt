================================================================================
MRF SOFTBALL LEAGUE - OFFICIAL LINEUP, ROTATION & PLAYING TIME RULES
================================================================================
League: MRF Fastpitch Softball
Category: Mandatory Defensive Rotation, Bench Sitting Limits, and Lineup Rules

1. BENCH ROTATION & SITTING RULES:
- Any player CANNOT be benched for two (2) consecutive innings in a game.
- No player should sit twice before every player on the team sits once.
- Rule Intent: Fair and equal bench rotation. Players cannot sit back-to-back innings, and bench time must be distributed across the entire roster before anyone sits a second time.

2. MANDATORY INFIELD & OUTFIELD ROTATION REQUIREMENTS:
- All girls must play at least one inning at an INFIELD position and at least one inning at an OUTFIELD position by the end of the 4th inning.
- Eligible Infield Positions (all count as Infield):
  * Pitcher (P)
  * Catcher (C)
  * First Base (1B)
  * Second Base (2B)
  * Shortstop (SS)
  * Third Base (3B)
- Eligible Outfield Positions:
  * Left Field (LF)
  * Center Field (CF)
  * Right Field (RF)

3. LINEUP CHECKLIST & COMPLIANCE SUMMARY:
- Inning-by-Inning Check: Ensure no player sits for 2 consecutive innings.
- Rotation Balance Check: Ensure no player sits a 2nd inning until all teammates have sat at least 1 inning.
- 4th Inning Milestone Check: By the conclusion of the 4th inning, verify that every player on the roster has played at least one inning in the infield (P, C, 1B, 2B, SS, 3B) and at least one inning in the outfield (LF, CF, RF).
